I am a researcher with a strong foundation in **computer systems**, now focusing on **large language models (LLMs), multimodal learning, model merging, agentic AI, and trustworthy AI**, with particular interests in **reinforcement learning, preference optimization, and capability transfer**.

I hold a Master’s degree in Data Science from the University of Pennsylvania (GPA 4.0/4.0), where I completed coursework in machine learning, computer systems, algorithms, GPU computing, and artificial intelligence.

I have co-authored papers published at **SOSP 2025** and **ICSE 2026**, which provide a strong systems foundation for my current research. I am also the first author of **three papers under review at ICLR 2027**, covering **RL-based capability transfer for visual reasoning, cross-task model merging, and provenance for LLM agents**.

My research experience spans low-level systems and runtime analysis—such as automated syscall testing for QEMU and differential testing frameworks—as well as recent work on **LLM post-training, multimodal reasoning, model merging, preference optimization, and agent watermarking and provenance**. My background in computer systems and program analysis informs my approach to these problems, particularly in understanding the **efficiency, robustness, and reliability** of LLM-based and multimodal AI systems.

Please learn more about me from my [CV](files/CV_JI_2027.pdf). Feel free to reach out if you would like to discuss research ideas or potential collaborations.
